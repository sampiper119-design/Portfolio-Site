Interactive & Creative Coding

WordBlocks Game

An interactive, web-based word puzzle game that combines the tile-sliding mechanics of 2048 with the word-building of Scrabble. The game challenges players to slide letter tiles across a 6x6 grid using keyboard or swipe controls to form words before the board fills up. It features custom scoring algorithms, wildcard tiles, grid-clearing power-ups, and an automated endgame sweep.

Visual & Graphic Design

Alphabet Project

A typographic and visual design study inspired by Pentagram’s Osh Gallery Alphabet. Guided by Gestalt principles, this project explores the boundaries between legibility and abstraction— deconstructing all 26 letterforms into an evolving narrative story. Early letters begin as minimalist, geometric foundations, and progressively escalate into more intricate, motif-driven visual identities.

About Me

I grew up programming— spending my free time experimenting with new ideas and languages, and challenging myself to create increasingly intricate projects. Along the way, I developed a deep interest in visual design— creating posters, logos, and other forms of branding collateral for school clubs and personal works.

I've always been drawn to the subtle details that elevate a digital experience. Designing from a more human-centered perspective allows me to ensure I create work that is not only functional and intuitive, but thoughtful, engaging, and visually compelling.

Studying New Media Interactive Development gives me a way to bridge programming and design into meaningful creative solutions— building programs that help to communicate something important.
